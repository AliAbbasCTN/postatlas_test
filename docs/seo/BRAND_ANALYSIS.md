# Brand analysis: keep PostAtlas?

## Recommendation: KEEP "PostAtlas". Do not rename for keyword reasons.
Confidence: moderate. The name is distinctive and fits the product; the two things that could change this decision (trademark and domain availability) could **not** be checked from here and must be checked before any spend (see "Before you commit").

## Evidence
| Question | Finding | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| Is the exact name already used as a link-building brand? | Exact-phrase searches for "PostAtlas", "postatlas.com" and "PostAtlas guest post / backlink" returned no company or product with that name. Results were unrelated atlases and postcode books. A search index is not a registry, so this shows low visible use, not absence. | VERIFIED (search), limited |
| Is it distinctive? | A coined compound: "post" (guest post, placement) + "atlas" (a map, a catalogue; fits "find the right sites"). It describes the idea without being a generic phrase, which makes it easier to protect and to rank for as a brand than a keyword name. | ESTIMATE |
| Is it memorable and easy to spell? | Two common English words, 9 letters, one obvious spelling, easy to say aloud. | ESTIMATE |
| Nearest brands that could cause confusion | **Search Atlas** (LinkGraph's SEO platform, appears in link-building content), **Posticy** (a guest post and link marketplace), **PostRunner** (a guest-posting tool), **Backlink Bazaar** (an app). "Search Atlas" is the closest in sound and in industry. | VERIFIED (appear in search results); degree of confusion is an ESTIMATE |
| Exact-brand search demand | None measurable: the brand is new. Brand demand is created by use, not by naming. | NOT AVAILABLE (no tool); expected to be near zero today |

A keyword-style domain gives no ranking guarantee in 2026, and a descriptive name is harder to trademark. | ESTIMATE |

## Why not rename
1. A rename costs links, mentions and trust that do not exist yet, but it also costs every future page, email and listing. The benefit is speculative.
2. Search engines do not reward exact-match brand words in 2026 the way they once did; relevance of content and links decides rankings.
3. No candidate below is **verified** as better: all of their availability is unknown, and several are descriptive (weaker trademark).

## Before you commit (external tasks)
1. Search the trademark registers that matter to you (for example USPTO, UKIPO, the Pakistan IPO and WIPO Global Brand Database) for "PostAtlas" and "Post Atlas" in the relevant classes (software / SaaS, advertising and marketing services). Take advice before filing.
2. Check that a suitable domain (.com first) is free or acquirable, and that the matching social handles are free. Do not assume `postatlas-test.vercel.app` will be the production address.
3. Compare "PostAtlas" and "Search Atlas" side by side with a few users. If people mix them up, add a clear descriptor line to the brand (for example "PostAtlas: guest post and backlink marketplace"), which the site already does in its title and homepage H1.
