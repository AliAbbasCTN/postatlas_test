# Keyword research

## Read this first: what is and is not verified
**No keyword-tool data was available** (no Ahrefs, Semrush, Keyword Planner or Search Console access), so **no search volume or keyword-difficulty number is given**. Inventing them would mislead. Instead each keyword is classified by:
* **Intent** (transactional / commercial / informational / navigational), read from the kind of pages that rank in the results that were returned (VERIFIED for the pattern, ESTIMATE for the conclusion);
* **Realistic opportunity** (ESTIMATE): *High* = few direct competitors would target it and PostAtlas has real content for it; *Medium*; *Low* = dominated by large marketplaces or affiliate roundups, do not target now.

**To fill in real numbers (free path):** connect Google Search Console after launch (real impressions per query), and use Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account, ranges only). **Paid path:** Ahrefs or Semrush keyword explorer on the list below.

## Deliberately not built
* **"On-page SEO", "off-page SEO", "web development services" pages.** PostAtlas does not sell those services, and the instructions forbid inventing product features. `/check` already covers a factual on-page observation and `/blog` can host educational articles later.
* **"X alternative" pages** (Collaborator/Adsy/WhitePress alternative). They need a real catalogue and verified comparison facts. Adding them now would be thin or unfair. Revisit once the marketplace has inventory.
* **Mass "X blogs that accept guest posts" pages** like Adsy's. They would be near-duplicates and low value; category pages are generated only when 3+ approved sites exist.
* **City / country pages**, **AI-written blog articles**: no real content behind them.
